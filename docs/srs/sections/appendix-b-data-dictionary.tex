\section{Data Dictionary and Data Model}\label{app:data}

\subsection{Data Model}

\figref{fig:datamodel} shows the entities the requirements name and the relationships between them. Attributes are the ones that appear in requirements; the data dictionary below gives their meaning and type. The Inventory is the Resource Type and Resource entities together with the Holds and allocations on them; the Ledger is the Account and Transaction entities. Neither holds the other's information (\BR{transactions}).
