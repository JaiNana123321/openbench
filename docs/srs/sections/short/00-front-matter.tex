\noindent\textbf{How to read this document.} Section~\ref{sec:vision} is the Vision and Scope statement; Sections~\ref{sec:intro}--\ref{sec:nfr} are the specification itself, and the appendices hold the glossary, the data and analysis models, the use cases, the inspection report, and the traceability table. Functional requirements carry dot-notation identifiers such as \textbf{Draft.Submit.Lock}; use cases UC-$n$ are listed in Appendix~\ref{app:usecases}. Capitalized words such as Request, Hold, and Loan are defined in the glossary, Appendix~\ref{app:glossary}, which is generated from the team's shared vocabulary file. A longer edition of this specification with the complete requirement set, the business rules, and the use cases written out in full is kept in the project repository; identifiers are the same in both.
