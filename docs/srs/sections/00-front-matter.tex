\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{How to read this document.} Section~\ref{sec:vision} is the Vision and Scope statement. Sections~\ref{sec:intro}--\ref{sec:nfr} are the specification itself, and the appendices hold the glossary, the data and analysis models, the use cases, the inspection report, and the traceability table. Functional requirements carry dot-notation identifiers such as \textbf{Draft.Submit.Afford}; business rules are numbered BR-$n$; use cases UC-$n$ are in Appendix~\ref{app:usecases}. Capitalized words such as Request, Hold, and Loan are defined terms; the glossary in Appendix~\ref{app:glossary} is generated from the team's shared vocabulary file.
