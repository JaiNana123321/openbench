\subsection{Decisions Behind the Requirements}

The following design decisions are recorded as architecture decision records in the project repository under \texttt{docs/adr/} and are the rationale for the rules named: Holds bounded by the Objection Window (\BR{hold-bounded}, \BR{objection-window}); the two-part Account with expiring Grants and carried Earnings (\BR{grant-expiry}, \BR{spend-order}); priority bought with a premium rather than granted by rank (\BR{priority-premium}); and Matched lines costing nothing (\BR{matched-free}, \BR{day-rate}).
